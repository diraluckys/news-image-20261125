# The World in One Day — Wednesday 25 November 2026

A single image summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 world news: 35 stories
across 7 regions — two tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz, the
Asian tech rout (KOSPI −6.16%, Nikkei −2.78%), and the US unveiling a new
tariff plan — plus a UTC timeline, a numbers strip, a pinned world map, and
a DATE CHECK box that dates every adjacent-day story honestly. Every story
was verified against primary sources (Reuters, Wall Street Journal, Seoul
Economic Daily, BBC, and others) for the 25 November 2026 make.fun
news-image competition.

The image is `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB). `main.ts` (Node 22,
stdlib only, no network) copies `entry.png` to the output directory
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`) byte for byte.
